Letter Quest Picture Pack

  1. Tap Find to search Google Images, then long-press a picture and save it.
  2. Tap Add and choose the picture you saved.
  3. When you're done, tap Send pack and send the file to George's tablet.
  4. On the tablet, open Letter Quest, go to Pictures and tap Load picture pack.

Pictures stay on this phone until you send the pack. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
